About SafeZone

  • SafeZone is an ESP32-powered smart livestock monitoring system designed for precision herd management.
  • Each collar tracks GPS position in real time and activates a built-in deterrent system when a cow exits its safe zone.
  • The system operates across three zones — Zone 1 (safe), Zone 2 (warning), and Zone 3 (critical) — with automated responses at each level.
  • Email and push notifications keep you informed instantly, even when you are away from the farm.
  • SafeZone is built for farmers who need reliable, autonomous protection without manual intervention.

Reading This Dashboard

  • The hourly timeline shows breach events per hour today — use it to identify high-risk time windows.
  • The daily history chart reveals weekly patterns to help you improve fence placement and deterrent settings.
  • Tap the bell icon to review collar alerts, zone breach logs, and system notifications.
  • Open the navigation menu to access real-time tracking, farm setup, fence editing, or alert customization.
  • The dashboard refreshes automatically — all data reflects the latest collar and farm activity.
